    The road to the platinum

    Eight stages, in the order to play them, built from the official trophy descriptions: the chapter and named-mission trophies fix the story order, the stranger finales form their own block, and the counters and collectibles fill the stages after the finale. The official list holds no online trophies — every step below is single-player. Trophies flagged Missable are the ones whose descriptions tie them to a moment or a choice that may not come twice; everything else can be cleaned up in free roam.

    Missables: the five to plan around

    These flags are derived from the official descriptions — a trophy is flagged when its description ties it to a single story moment, a choice, or a scripted first attempt. Each card quotes the official description the flag was derived from, and the full guide explains when the moment comes and how to protect it (a manual save before story decisions is the cheapest insurance in the game).

    Sources & method

    The trophy names, descriptions and grades in this guide are the official PlayStation 5 list for Grand Theft Auto VI, as published by PlayStation and mirrored by the two trophy databases below before this guide was written; both mirrors agree on every name, description and grade. Every trophy's guide is written for this entry and names its sources beside the trophy. The roadmap order and the missable flags are this guide's own work, derived from the official descriptions — the reasoning is stated in each flagged trophy's guide. Links open only when you follow them; the app itself fetches nothing and works with the network off.
